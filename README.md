# Built with Claude

Just a guy trying to make a dream.

I'm a solo builder, not a career developer. Until recently, the ideas in my head stayed in my head — I was never going to hand-copy thousands of lines of code into folders to see them come to life. Working with Claude changed that. Now I design, build, test, and ship real apps.

## What I've built so far

My working repos are private while the projects are in development, but here's what's in them:

- **DrawWatch** — a lottery draw checker, live on the Google Play Store today. My first proof that an idea in my head could become something real that strangers can download and use.
- **A home inspection app** (Flutter/Dart) — actively in development, built to make inspections faster and better documented.
- **A 4v1 asymmetric multiplayer game** (Roblox/Luau) — a monster-hunt experience, currently my most active project.
- **A life visualizer** (Flutter/Dart) — a "life in weeks" app for seeing your whole life at a glance, with an emphasis on polish and feel.
- **Additional experiments** in Dart and TypeScript as I learn what people actually want to use.

## Why this repo exists

This is my public landing spot. The goal is simple: keep turning ideas into things people want to use or enjoy, and open-source the pieces of that journey that can help others do the same.

If you're curious about any of the projects above, open an issue and say hi.
