## 🤝 How I work

One person, one AI pair programmer, five projects. Claude handles the heavy lifting on code; I bring the ideas, the taste, the testing, and the stubbornness to ship. Every project in this list went from "thought in my head" to "running software" that way.

## 🎯 Why this repo exists

This is my public landing spot. The goal is simple: keep turning ideas into things people want to use or enjoy, and open-source the pieces of that journey that can help others do the same.

If you're curious about any of the projects above, open an issue and say hi. 👋
