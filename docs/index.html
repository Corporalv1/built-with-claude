▸How this works

One person, one AI, five projects. Claude does the heavy lifting on code. I bring the ideas, the taste, the testing, and the stubbornness to actually ship.

I was never going to hand copy thousands of lines of code from tutorials into folders. Way too much work. Claude didn't make me a developer. It made me the director of a tiny dev team that happens to not be human. Turns out that's enough to ship.

The working repos stay private while things are in development, but the goal is to share the parts of the journey that can help other people build their own somedays.
